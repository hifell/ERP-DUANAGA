B-AUTOMATE

Brand Identity & Style Guide

Version: 1.0 Date: September 2026 Status: Official Brand Guidelines

1. BRAND OVERVIEW

1.1 Brand Positioning

B-Automate adalah brand otomatisasi bisnis yang membantu perusahaan mengelola operasional dengan sistem terintegrasi. Fokus utama adalah efisiensi, profesionalisme, dan solusi teknologi yang mudah dipahami.

1.2 Tagline

"Bisnismu jalan, sistemnya otomatis."

1.3 Brand Personality

Attribute Description
Professional Tampilan bersih, terstruktur, business-focused
Modern Tech-forward, mengikuti perkembangan teknologi
Accessible Solusi yang bisa dipahami non-technical user
Reliable Trusted partner untuk transformation digital
Efficient Fokus pada hasil dan penghematan

1.4 Brand Values

✓ EFFICIENCY     — Hemat waktu, hemat biaya
✓ AUTOMATION     — Kurangi manual, maksimal teknologi
✓ GROWTH         — Skalabilitas bisnis
✓ SIMPLICITY     — Sistem yang mudah dipahami
✓ RELIABILITY    — Partner jangka panjang

8. VOICE & TONE

8.1 Voice Attributes

Attribute Definition Example
Professional Business-appropriate, expert "Solusi ERP terintegrasi"
Clear Easy to understand, no jargon "Hemat 40% waktu"
Confident Assured, knowledgeable "Sistem yang proven"
Helpful Solution-oriented "Membantu bisnis Anda tumbuh"
Modern Tech-forward, contemporary "Otomatisasi, bukan manual"

8.2 Tone Guidelines

Situation Tone Example
Marketing Confident, benefit-focused "Hemat waktu, naikkan revenue"
Technical Precise, clear "API endpoint: /webhook/lead"
Support Helpful, patient "Kami bantu sampai sistem jalan"
Casual Friendly, approachable "Jadi lebih gampang deh"

8.3 Writing Guidelines

Do:

✓ Use active voice
✓ Keep sentences short (15-20 words)
✓ Focus on benefits, not features
✓ Use numbers: "40%" not "forty percent"
✓ Include action verbs

Don't:

✗ Use excessive technical jargon
✗ Write paragraphs instead of bullet points
✗ Use passive voice unnecessarily
✗ Overuse exclamation marks
✗ Use informal slang in formal contexts

8.4 Sample Copy

Headline Formats
